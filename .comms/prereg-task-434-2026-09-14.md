## 0 · The question, and what would falsify the answer

Not "what if I assume X bp" (TASK-433 answered that) but: **can this capital actually be moved in
these names without participation making TASK-433 irrelevant?** The number that matters is one
scalar per universe — the **maximum AUM before the 95th percentile of order participation exceeds
3 %** (Lucas 2026-09-11) — plus the share of orders that breach 1 % / 3 % / 5 %.

**This is a participation ceiling, not true capacity.** It certifies nothing about market impact.
Until the second half exists — an impact formula, a spread/volatility source, pre-registered
parameters — every figure this task publishes carries the label **`CAPACITY_NOT_CERTIFIED`**.
The label is part of the output, printed beside the number, never a footnote.

The result is falsified as *participation evidence* if any of these fails:

- **F1** the capacity drive's wealth series is not the accredited `russell/base` series under the
  seal `provenance` already uses — `book_sha256` (`hash_pandas_object` over values AND index,
  `provenance.py:202`) equal to `12e478f9752e51902ec268fd499cefc28dd7bb62c50ef37d6c77afb273fe83da`,
  `calendar_sha256` equal to `63fda57fbb1f4853917c98ca9175c63c7cd309c87f2994c252d818b469bf714f`
  over exactly 814 marks — plus `ledger.by_step` sleeve totals and `cost_bp_effective_by_sleeve`
  equal to the original run's. Raw pickle bytes are NOT the criterion: the container may
  serialise differently while the economic series is the same, and the seal is on the numbers.
  The sidecar is an observer, never a participant;
- **F2** ADV is not the lab's one definition (`(close * volume).rolling(20).mean()`,
  `redesign_lab.py:323` = `build_adv_panel.py`), or it is read at `t` instead of `t-1`;
- **F3** a zero / missing volume becomes an ADV of 0 (infinite participation landing quietly in the
  top bucket) instead of "unknown";
- **F4** the AUM figure is read on the covered subset as if it were the universe — see §3, the
  conservative P95 must be finite for a number to exist;
- **F5** the scale-homogeneity property (§2) is not demonstrated before the rescaling is used;
- **F6** the participation unit is not the execution footprint (§3), or the unit changes after
  the first table is seen.

## 2 · What has to be built (and what must NOT change)

The accredited artefacts hold fills **per settle and per sleeve** (`ledger.by_step[t][sleeve]
.filled_dollars`), not per name. Per-order participation needs a **fill sidecar**:
`(settle_date, sleeve, tranche, ticker, side, filled_dollars)` for every filled event.

- `experiments/engine_backtest.py` gains an *optional* sink for fill records. Default off; the
  wealth path is untouched. The module is inside the 433 books' recorded identity — allowed now
  under #96's rule, and F1 is the proof that only logging was added. Test: with the sink on, on a
  synthetic panel, NAV/ledger/decisions equal the sink-off run exactly.
- **Scale homogeneity as an executable property (F5), before F1.** On a deterministic synthetic
  panel, drive at capital `1` and at capital `K` (K = 7.3, 1000, 1e6): `NAV_K / K == NAV_1`,
  `fill_K / K == fill_1` per record, identical `(settle, sleeve, tranche, ticker, side)` sets and
  identical order of events, to a tolerance of `1e-9` relative (floating point, not semantics).
  If this fails, "one drive → 100 k / 500 k / 1 M" is dead and three drives are needed; the prereg
  says so now. Grounds for expecting it to pass: engine capital is `1.0`, `whole_shares = false`,
  costs are proportional bp; production also keeps orders fractional (`portfolio_v9.py:546`,
  `:577` — `whole_shares` is display-only).
- `experiments/capacity.py` (new, the board's `Files:`): reads the sidecar + ADV panel, builds
  the execution footprint (§3), computes participation, buckets, the two aggregates, the AUM
  scan, the coverage ratios; reads the real sheet through the same path.
- `experiments/test_capacity.py`: synthetic sidecar + synthetic ADV; every rule in §3 pinned by a
  mutation (ADV read at `t`, a zero-ADV row, a missing ticker counted as covered, a non-monotone
  scan, footprint built per micro-fill instead of per `(settle, sleeve, ticker)`).
- No new definition of ADV. `capacity.py` imports the builder's function; a test asserts it
  equals the lab's `P.ADV_USD` line on a synthetic panel.

## 3 · Measurement rules — frozen

- **Unit of participation = the execution footprint**, not the micro-fill: fills are aggregated
  by `(settle, sleeve, ticker)` with **signed netting** across tranches (a buy and a sell of the
  same name in the same settle net to one order), and the participation of that footprint is
  `|net_dollars| / ADV`. **Gross** (sum of absolute micro-fills) is printed as a sensitivity
  column, never as the headline. Today production emits one row per `(ticker, sleeve)` and a
  single tranche (the 2026-09-04 sheet: 30 orders, 30 distinct, `tranche = 0`), so net and gross
  coincide; the rule is frozen for the day they do not, and the production netting rule is read
  from `portfolio_v9.py` at that time, not assumed here.
- **ADV20 is ex-ante**: the canonical series `(close * volume).rolling(20).mean()` read at
  **`t-1` = the market bar immediately before the footprint's settle bar** on the panel's own
  index — a Monday fill reads Friday's ADV; a calendar day is never the unit. The day's own
  realised volume never enters its denominator. If that previous bar does not carry a full window
  of 20 observations for the ticker (`rolling(20)` with the default `min_periods` → NaN), ADV is
  **unknown** — the window is never shortened, nothing is forward-filled. Ex-post (`t` included)
  is not computed; if anyone wants it, it is a new pre-registration and cannot be sold as
  available capacity.
- **participation = |net_dollars| / ADV20_USD(ticker, t-1)**. ADV in adjusted-close dollars is
  fine for a ratio of dollars (both legs adjusted the same way, as `build_adv_panel.py` states);
  the tape's raw dollar volume is NOT claimed.
- **Thresholds 1 % / 3 % / 5 %** (normal / warn / do-not-trade), as the board fixes them.
- **Capital levels 100 k, 500 k, 1 M USD**: one drive at `capital = 1.0`, three rescalings
  `participation(C) = fill_frac × C / ADV`, licensed only by the F5 property.
- **P95 is one discrete, conservative order statistic, everywhere**: sort the N participations
  ascending and take the observation at rank `ceil(0.95 × N)` (1-based) — `numpy.quantile(...,
  method="higher")` is NOT it when `+∞` is present under some implementations, so the rank is
  computed explicitly and the same function serves the descriptive P95, the conservative P95, the
  AUM scan and the real sheet. No linear interpolation anywhere. With `+∞` entries this is
  exact: the P95 is finite iff strictly fewer than `N − ceil(0.95 × N) + 1` footprints are
  unknown, which is why "exactly 5 % unknown" cannot land on either side by accident. Pinned by
  a test with N = 20, 21, 100 and 1 / 2 unknowns.
- **Unknown ADV is fail-closed for the ceiling.** A footprint whose ticker is absent from the
  panel or whose ADV at `t-1` is NaN has participation **unknown**. Two P95s are computed:
  *descriptive* (covered footprints only, printed with both coverage ratios) and
  *conservative* (unknown participation counts as `+∞`). The AUM ceiling uses the conservative
  one: **if more than 5 % of the relevant footprints (by count OR by notional) have unknown ADV,
  the conservative P95 is `+∞` at every capital level and the result is `NOT MEASURABLE`** — the
  descriptive figure is still printed, under that label, never as a ceiling. The 80 % coverage
  threshold of v1 survives only as a descriptive flag.
- **The two aggregates**, per universe, per capital level, per sleeve and total:
  1. % of covered footprints with participation > 1 %, > 3 %, > 5 % (plus the unknown share);
  2. **max AUM (participation ceiling)**: the largest C on a geometric grid (10 k … 100 M, ×1.25)
     such that conservative P95(participation at C) ≤ 3 % — reported with the full P95 curve so
     the scan is auditable, bracketed (largest passing, smallest failing), labelled
     `CAPACITY_NOT_CERTIFIED`.
- **Real sheet**: the 30 footprints of 2026-09-04 against ADV at `planned - 1` and at
  `exec_date - 1`; both printed. One sheet is one point: it goes in the table, it does not get a
  percentile of its own.
- Period: the accredited window only (2010-06-28 → 2026-08-26, 814 settles), research +
  validation, `holdout` declaration `fc38929b64e8`; nothing in `live`.

## 5 · What this task does NOT claim

- Not market impact, not true capacity: `CAPACITY_NOT_CERTIFIED` until the impact half exists
  (formula, spread/vol source, parameters — pre-registered separately). Endogenous cost must feed
  a *new* cost scenario in the 433 harness, not be read off this table.
- Not a PIT liquidity statement: `fixed_map` sectors, 56 % volume coverage, adjusted-close ADV.
- Not "Russell is less liquid than S&P" unless both universes are `MEASURABLE`; coverage is
  printed first, the comparison second, and only then.

## 7 · Pre-declared expectations (so the result can surprise us)

- At 100 k, the real sheet's 30 footprints (324–616 USD) sit far below 1 % of ADV for any name
  with a measurable ADV; breaches at 100 k would point at the ADV panel, not at the strategy.
- The ceiling is set by the stocks sleeve, not the ETF sleeve (ETF ADV is orders of magnitude
  larger); Russell's ceiling is expected below the S&P's — but Russell may well come out
  `NOT MEASURABLE` under the 5 % fail-closed rule, and that is an acceptable outcome of this
  task, not a failure of it.
- **Written now so it cannot be softened later:** if the **participation ceiling under this
  pre-registered rule** (conservative P95 ≤ 3 %, ex-ante ADV20, footprint unit) on Russell lands
  below ≈ 1 M USD, Block A's conclusion changes from "moderate edge" to **"moderate edge, not
  scalable on Russell by participation"**. "Participation ceiling" means exactly that — not the
  strategy's total capacity, which stays `CAPACITY_NOT_CERTIFIED` until impact is measured.
